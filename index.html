Method Overview

Target-Aware Mapping and Exploration

SKGS refines target-category Gaussians from historical observations, while SWMI guides future observations toward uncertain target regions. Together, they form a guide--observe--refine loop.

Precise Target Localization

Coarse-to-fine localization and PnP-based viewpoint alignment exploit virtual 3DGS renderings for reliable matching across arbitrary viewpoints.

Re-exploration and Navigation

When localization fails, re-exploration collects supplementary observations of previously seen candidates before final target navigation.

Real-World Deployment

The physical-robot demonstration video will be placed here.

TAGSNav was deployed on a modified Yuhesen FW-Mid Pro mobile chassis without additional training or fine-tuning. The experiments cover long-range observations, occlusion, similar distractors, and safety-constrained navigation.

Limitations

TAGSNav remains limited by the computational cost of online Gaussian optimization and mutual-information evaluation. Performance may also degrade under severe segmentation errors, extremely texture-poor targets, or incomplete geometry.
