Abstract

Instance Image Goal Navigation (IIN) is a fundamental task in embodied navigation that requires an agent to locate and navigate to a specific object instance in an unknown environment given a reference image. Its core challenge lies in recognizing the same target instance across viewpoints while distinguishing it from visually similar instances of the same category under sparse observations and computational constraints. Most existing methods use 2D semantic maps or scene graphs, with target appearance representations limited to observed viewpoints, restricting instance matching across arbitrary viewpoints. 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) offers a way to overcome this limitation by enabling viewpoint-aligned matching through novel-view rendering. However, optimization-based online 3DGS still struggles to preserve the reconstruction details needed for instance-level discrimination under sparse observations and limited optimization budgets. To this end, we propose TAGSNav, an online IIN framework coupling Incremental Semantic Keyframe-Guided Gaussian Splatting (SKGS) with Gaussian-uncertainty-driven Semantically Weighted Mutual Information (SWMI)-guided exploration in a closed mapping–exploration loop. This loop uses uncertainty to guide observations, improving online reconstruction quality in target-category regions with limited observations and a fixed optimization budget. Building on the resulting maps, coarse-to-fine localization and compensatory re-exploration reduce false and missed matches across arbitrary viewpoints. Viewpoint alignment is additionally performed when needed to verify localization results. On HM3D, it outperforms state-of-the-art methods in SR and SPL; physical-robot deployment without retraining further verifies Sim2Real generalization.

Method Overview

Overview of TAGSNav: the framework comprises a mapping–exploration loop that optimizes Gaussian regions associated with the target category, coarse-to-fine instance localization and viewpoint optimization, and re-exploration. Click the figure to view it at full size.

Target-Aware Mapping–Exploration Loop

TAGSNav couples Incremental Semantic Keyframe-Guided Gaussian Splatting (SKGS) with Semantically Weighted Mutual Information (SWMI)-guided exploration in a closed mapping–exploration loop. SKGS refines Gaussian regions associated with the target category using historical observations, while SWMI uses Gaussian uncertainty to guide the acquisition of informative observations. Together, they improve target-region reconstruction under limited observations and a fixed optimization budget.

Instance Localization and Re-exploration

Building on the resulting maps, TAGSNav performs coarse-to-fine instance localization using novel-view renderings, with viewpoint optimization and alignment applied when needed to verify localization results. Compensatory re-exploration gathers additional observations of previously seen candidates when localization fails. These components reduce false and missed matches across arbitrary viewpoints.

Complementary Improvements for IIN

TAGSNav addresses instance image goal navigation from two complementary perspectives: the mapping–exploration loop improves the quality of the target-category 3D representations used for matching, while localization and re-exploration improve the reliability of the matching process. Together, these improvements support more reliable target-instance localization and navigation in unknown environments.

Real-World Deployment

The physical-robot demonstration video will be placed here.

TAGSNav was deployed on a modified Yuhesen FW-Mid Pro mobile chassis without additional training or fine-tuning. The experiments cover long-range observations, occlusion, similar distractors, and safety-constrained navigation.

Limitations

TAGSNav remains limited by the computational cost of online Gaussian optimization and mutual-information evaluation. Performance may also degrade under severe segmentation errors, extremely texture-poor targets, or incomplete geometry.
